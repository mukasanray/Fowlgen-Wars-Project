# Prompt do Logo Oficial — Galo Guerreiro

Documentação do prompt para recriar a imagem `logo_oficial.jpg` (mascote estilo esports).

---

## 1. Prompt principal (inglês)

> Funciona melhor em geradores de imagem (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, DALL·E, etc.).

```
Esports mascot logo, square 1:1 composition, centered front-facing emblem on a flat solid deep royal blue background (#0B2478). An anthropomorphic white rooster warrior with an eagle-like fierce head, angry determined expression: narrow aggressive eyes with dark navy eyebrows and heavy eyeliner, small orange-yellow pointed beak, a tiny red "X"-shaped battle scar on the cheek (viewer's right side). A tall jagged stylized bright red comb on top of the head, shaped like a flame/mohawk with sharp points. Fluffy cream-white feathered body and muscular feathered arms with jagged feather tufts and soft beige shading.

He wears shiny golden-yellow knight armor: a pointed V-shaped chest plate with a bold chunky yellow letter "F" in the center (outlined in dark brown/black, with beveled faceted edges), plus angular gold shoulder pauldrons.

His left hand (viewer's left) grips a large steel broadsword held diagonally upward and tilted to the left, going beyond the top-left of the emblem: silver-gray blade with light blue-white faceted reflections, a golden diamond-shaped crossguard, a brown leather-wrapped grip, and a golden pommel. His right hand (viewer's right) is a clenched feathered fist held at waist level.

Two large symmetrical white wings spread wide behind him, with cream and light-beige inner shading and sharp spiky feather tips pointing outward and upward.

Behind the character, a shield-shaped badge with a pointed bottom: vertical olive-green and dark-brown wooden planks, with a thin golden-yellow border.

Art style: vector illustration, flat cel-shading, thick bold black outlines around every shape, clean sharp edges, high contrast, vibrant saturated colors (red, gold yellow, white, royal blue), game logo / esports team mascot style, symmetrical balanced composition, no text besides the "F" on the armor, no gradients on the background, high detail, crisp, 4k.
```

| Stable Diffusion | CFG 7–8, sampler DPM++ 2M Karras, 30–40 steps |
