# Paid advertising source list - snapshot verified 2026-08-26

Every entry was checked by direct fetch on the snapshot date.

Statuses:

- **Active**: dated item ≤ ~3 months old at check.
- **Stale**: reachable, newest dated item > ~6 months.
- **Unverified**: fetch blocked/failed. Reason recorded, never assumed dead.
- **Dead**: parked, for-sale, or non-resolving. Only after every known surface failed.

"not stated" means the source publishes no audience figure - do not invent one.

"What they're selling" discloses the incentive behind the content. Almost nothing in this field is conflict-free, so the flag is for disclosure, not disqualification.

Re-verify anything here before recommending it (see SKILL.md, Freshness check).

Section order below is a catalogue, not a priority order. What earns a slot is ranked by efficiency - value per minute of attention - in SKILL.md, _Rank by efficiency_: a daily roundup or a release-note skim outranks a podcast under a tight budget, and that flips the moment the podcast rides on a commute.
