## How to use this list

- Match the Topic column to the user's bought channels and named themes. A depth-focused user gets 3–5 people on their themes, not the whole table.
- Rank by cross-surface recurrence - the same person as podcast guest, speaker, and community lead predicts continued relevance better than any follower count. Never rank by audience size; a slot is worth what the person's output changes in the reader's account, not what their reach is.
- Before linking any person's personal domain, confirm the page content is about advertising: two entries above (Ben Heath, Nik Sharma) have same-name domains serving unrelated businesses.
- To find people beyond this table, work the discovery surfaces in efficiency order - named, dated people confirmed per fetch:

  `podcast guest archives > conference speaker rosters > community leadership pages > tool-vendor benchmark posts > social profiles`

  - Guest archive: returns several people per page, already dated and topic-tagged.
  - Speaker roster: returns names with no recency.
  - Benchmark post: mentions one or two in passing.
  - Social profile: blocks the fetch entirely.

- Cheapest is not the same order: the benchmark post is a single fetch and the thinnest return, while the guest archive that leads on efficiency usually costs a feed lookup first. Re-rank against the user's own position - a community they already belong to outperforms every public surface here, and a conference they already attend turns its roster into a live one.
